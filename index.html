About GrooAI

A small studio with an engineer’s and a teacher’s habits.

GrooAI was founded by Changwoo Song, an AI instructor and software developer based in Daejeon. He teaches generative AI practice at universities and institutions, and builds automation and AI applications for the people he teaches. That loop — teaching, then shipping — is how the studio works.

Engagements are deliberately small and concrete: a workshop that leaves a team able to use the tools on its own work, or a pipeline that removes a repetitive task from someone’s week. Everything we build is meant to be handed over and operated by the client’s own team.

그루에이아이는 송창우 대표가 운영하는 1인 AI 스튜디오입니다. 대학·기관·기업에서 생성형 AI 실무를 가르치고, 그 현장에서 나온 문제를 자동화와 소프트웨어로 직접 만듭니다.

Services

Three things, done properly.

Each engagement starts from the work a team already does — and ends with something they can run themselves.

교육, 자동화, 개발. 필요한 부분만 정확하게 맡습니다.

01

AI Education & Workshops

생성형 AI·LLM 실무 교육

Instructor-led sessions for universities, public institutions and companies, built around the participants’ own tasks rather than a feature tour.

  • Prompt and context design for real work
  • LLM workflows for research, documents and knowledge management
  • AI-assisted development and office automation
02

AI Automation & Agents

업무 자동화 파이프라인 · 맞춤형 에이전트

Repetitive processes turned into reliable pipelines, and agents that handle a defined job with the tools and data a team already has.

  • Document intake, summarising and routing
  • Data collection, reporting and scheduled jobs
  • Agent workflows connected to existing services
03

Applied AI Software

데이터·문서 기반 실용 AI 애플리케이션

Small, focused applications that make a team’s own documents and data answerable and usable — built to run without a large platform behind them.

  • Retrieval and question answering over internal documents
  • Tools for research, writing and structured data
  • Prototypes that go into real use, not demos

How we work

Understand, build small, hand over.

Short cycles, working software, and no dependency on us afterwards.

Understand the work

We start with the task as it is actually done today, including the parts that are hard to explain on paper.

현장의 실제 업무 흐름부터 확인합니다.

Build the smallest version

A narrow, working version goes into use early, so the team can tell us what is wrong while it still costs little to change.

작게 만들어 빨리 써 보고, 그 피드백으로 고칩니다.

Hand it over

Documentation, training and a clear owner inside the team, so the result keeps working without us in the room.

문서와 교육을 남기고, 팀이 직접 운영할 수 있게 인계합니다.
